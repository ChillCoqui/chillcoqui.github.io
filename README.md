# ChillCoqui

### Making every genre cozy.

Hi! I'm the independent game developer behind **ChillCoqui**.

I create games with a simple goal: to explore how different genres—even those not usually considered relaxing—can become more cozy, welcoming, and chill.

For me, cozy games are not limited to one particular style or genre. A game can still offer challenge, progression, discovery, and satisfying mechanics while giving players a warm and comfortable experience.

---

## Paws & Seek: Animal Finder

**Paws & Seek: Animal Finder** is a cozy hidden-object game where players search for adorable dogs and cats across increasingly challenging rounds.

It combines the familiar hidden-object format with:

- Relaxing and colorful visuals
- Quick, approachable rounds
- High-score progression
- Achievements
- A more accessible **Big Mode**
- Rare bonus animals that can grant an extra life

## My Approach

I want to create games that feel chill and cozy without removing what makes their genres fun.

Whether a game is based on hidden objects, puzzles, action, strategy, or something completely different, I believe there is always room to make the experience more cozy.

That is what I am exploring through ChillCoqui—one game at a time.
